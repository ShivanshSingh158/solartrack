## Financial Payback & Appliance Load Handling for Dual System

### 1. What Can an ~24–27 kWh/Day Dual System Power?
With **24 to 27 units generated daily**, a dual system completely eliminates typical household electricity bills even with heavy summer loads:
- **Two 1.5-Ton Inverter ACs** running 6–8 hours during peak daytime: Consumes ~12–14 units.
- **Rooftop Water Pump (1 HP)** for 1.5 hours: Consumes ~1.2 units.
- **Washing Machine & Refrigerator**: Consumes ~2.5 units.
- **Lighting, Fans, Wi-Fi & TV**: Consumes ~3–4 units.
- **Net Grid Export**: You still export **3 to 6 surplus units daily** to the DISCOM under net-metering.

### 2. Dual System Capital Payback
- **Additional 3 kW System Turnkey Cost:** ~₹1,50,000 – ₹1,65,000
- **PM Surya Ghar Subsidy for 2nd / Expanded Phase:** ~₹40,000 – ₹50,000 (depending on scheme tier/approval)
- **Net Additional Investment:** ~₹1,05,000 – ₹1,15,000
- **Incremental Annual Revenue:** **+₹30,400 to +₹33,140 / year**
- **Incremental Payback Period:** **3.3 to 3.5 Years**
- **25-Year Additional Value Generated:** **₹7,00,000+**
